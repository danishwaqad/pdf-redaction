## Redact PDF online free: step by step

### Step 1 — Open the tool

Go to [RedactPDF](/tool). No signup. Drag your PDF into the upload area or click to browse.

### Step 2 — Review every page

Scroll through all pages. Note names, numbers, signatures, and headers/footers that repeat sensitive data.

### Step 3 — Mark redactions

Use three methods together:

| Method | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| Drag boxes | Lines, paragraphs, signatures, image regions |
| Text search | Names, case IDs, emails, repeated phrases |
| PII detect | Emails, phones, SSNs, credit cards, dates |

### Step 4 — Apply and download

Click **Apply Redactions**. Processing runs over HTTPS; the redacted PDF downloads to your device. Keep a copy of the certificate if your workflow requires audit trails.

### Step 5 — Verify

Search the output for known sensitive terms. Try copy-paste into a text editor. If text reappears, the redaction was cosmetic — not permanent.
