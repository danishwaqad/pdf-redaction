## Quick answer

You can **redact PDF on Android free** without installing a Play Store app: open **Chrome** (not an in-app browser), go to [RedactPDF](/tool), upload, mark, apply, verify. Skip "PDF editor" apps that draw black boxes — text usually stays searchable. Desktop browser is still better for hundred-page files; phones excel at urgent single-document jobs.

## Why Android app guides mislead you

Play Store "PDF redactor" apps often mean ads, unclear upload policies, and black rectangles that fail Ctrl+F. Google Drive markup is overlay-only. Screenshot + black bar does not touch the original PDF structure.

You need **permanent text removal** — same rules as desktop ([why blacking out fails](/blog/why-blacking-out-pdf-is-not-safe)).

## Real mobile scenario

A field auditor received a personnel PDF over WhatsApp. Opening it inside WhatsApp's browser, drawing black marks, and forwarding felt fast — until compliance ran search and found employee SSNs still in the file. The fix: open **Chrome** → [RedactPDF](/tool) → full apply + verify → share the **redacted** download from Files.

**Never redact inside Facebook, WhatsApp, or Gmail in-app browsers** — use Chrome directly.

## Step-by-step on Android

1. Open **Chrome** → [RedactPDF](/tool)
2. Tap upload → pick PDF from Files or Downloads (download Gmail attachments first)
3. Use mobile tabs: **PDF**, **Search**, **PII**
4. Pinch-zoom, draw boxes with finger
5. **Apply Redactions** on Wi-Fi for large files
6. Open output from Downloads → search for a known removed string

Rotate to landscape for wider canvas. Close other tabs on 4 GB phones. One document at a time saves memory.

## Verification on Android

Long-press in Adobe Reader or Chrome PDF viewer → find in document for strings you redacted. Or share to a text editor app and skim for leaks. Same three tests as desktop: search, select, paste.

## Security notes

Marking runs locally in the browser session. Apply sends the file once over HTTPS — not stored in a cloud inbox. Avoid highly classified work on shared or rooted devices. For SSN or HIPAA files, prefer desktop when possible — [SSN guide](/blog/how-to-redact-ssn-from-pdf), [medical HIPAA guide](/blog/how-to-redact-medical-records-pdf-hipaa).

## What not to do on Android

- Unknown apps with vague privacy policies
- Forwarding unredacted attachment "to fix later"
