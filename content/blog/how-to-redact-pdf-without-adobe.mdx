*Redact PDF without Adobe* is a practical search — not an anti-Adobe statement. Many professionals need **one job done once**: remove sensitive text before sharing. Paying for a full Acrobat license or navigating Creative Cloud login for a single exhibit is overkill. This guide covers a free workflow, honest limits, and when Adobe still earns its price.

## Why people search for Adobe alternatives

| Reason | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Cost | Acrobat Pro is a recurring subscription (~$240/year) |
| Friction | Install, updates, Creative Cloud account management |
| Scope | You need redaction — not signatures, forms, and prepress |
| Privacy | Teams want browser-local review with minimal retention |

Adobe Acrobat redact is mature software. The question is whether **your task** requires the entire suite today.
