## Can You Redact PDF in Microsoft Edge? The Hidden Danger

Edge lets you draw on PDFs with pen, highlighter, and text tools. You can place black shapes over sensitive lines. What Edge does **not** do is delete text from the PDF content stream.

| Edge action | What users expect | What often happens |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Draw black shape | Text is gone forever | Text remains searchable (Ctrl+F) |
| Highlight then print to PDF | Clean copy | Underlying layer may survive |
| Open in Edge, screenshot | Safe share | Original file unchanged |

Anyone can paste the document into Notepad or run search — and recover what looked hidden. That is not redaction.
