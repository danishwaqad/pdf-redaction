## Quick answer

You can **redact PDF without Adobe** in the browser: [RedactPDF](/tool) → mark (boxes, search, PII detect) → apply permanent redaction → verify with Ctrl+F. No Creative Cloud login, no ~$240/year for a one-off exhibit. ## Why people skip Acrobat

Paralegals, journalists, and office managers redact **quarterly** — FOIA letters, lease abstracts, HR exports. A full Acrobat seat for 2% of the job is hard to defend. Teams also want **no install** and **no standing cloud inbox** of sensitive PDFs.

Adobe Acrobat redact is mature. The question is whether **today's task** needs the entire suite.

## When Acrobat is still worth it

Bates numbering, redaction codes, Action Wizards, batch scripts, compare documents, accessible tagging at scale. High-volume repeat playbooks with IT support → Acrobat. Episodic privacy-sensitive redaction → free local tool.
