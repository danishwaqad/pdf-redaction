## Can You Redact PDF in Mac Preview? The Hidden Danger

Yes, Preview lets you annotate PDFs. You can add shapes, use Markup, even fill regions with black. What Preview does **not** do is true PDF redaction in the legal or security sense.

Preview edits are typically **annotations or drawing layers** sitting above the original content. The PDF's internal text operators — the instructions that place each character on the page — often remain intact. Anyone with Acrobat, a command-line extractor, or a careless copy-paste can recover what you thought you hid.

Mac users in legal, HR, healthcare, and journalism reach for Preview because it is already there. The hidden danger is assuming **"looks black on screen" equals "data removed."** Courts, regulators, and security teams increasingly test redacted files with search and extraction — Preview-only blackout routinely fails those tests.

| What Preview seems to do | What often still happens |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Black box over a name | Name remains in content stream |
| Markup on exported PDF | Underlying text searchable |
| Quick fix before email | Recipient recovers "redacted" data |

For the technical explanation, see [why blacking out a PDF is not safe](/blog/why-blacking-out-pdf-is-not-safe).

## Problem: Preview Black Box is Not Real Redaction

**Real redaction** permanently removes sensitive text (or rasterizes the page so text operators no longer exist). **Preview black box** is cosmetic masking — similar to dropping a sticker on a printed page while the original ink remains readable through backlighting.

### Common Preview workflows that fail

1. **Shapes tool** — Draw a filled black rectangle over a Social Security number. Visual: gone. File: often recoverable.
2. **Markup in Mail** — Black highlighter on a PDF attachment, then save. Still an overlay in many exports.
3. **Export to PDF from another app** — Black shapes from Pages or Word baked into a new PDF without deleting source text layers.

### The three tests Preview users skip

After any redaction, run these on your Mac before sharing:

| Test | How on Mac | Pass criteria |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Search | Cmd+F in Preview or Adobe Reader | Known sensitive strings return zero hits |
| Select | Drag to highlight under black areas | No text selects beneath marks |
| Paste | Copy all → paste into TextEdit | Redacted content absent |

If any test fails, you do not have redaction — you have decoration.

### When Preview is acceptable

Preview remains fine for **non-sensitive** markup: circling typos, signing forms, or highlighting passages for your own review. The moment the document leaves your trust boundary — court filing, GDPR response, client packet — switch to permanent redaction.
