People search **white out PDF** and **white out text PDF** when they want sensitive words to disappear into a clean white background — common on bank statements, medical forms, and legal exhibits where black bars look harsh. The technique is the same as blackout; only the **fill color** changes. The danger is identical: a white shape in Word or Edge is **not** removal. This guide shows how to white out text in a PDF **permanently**, free, online.

## White out vs black out — same security rules

| Term | User intent | Secure when… |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| White out | Hide text with white fill | Text deleted from file structure |
| Black out | Hide text with black fill | Text deleted from file structure |
| White rectangle overlay | Cover visually | **Unsafe** — Ctrl+F may still find text |

Color does not matter to attackers. **Permanent removal** matters.

## Wrong way to white out PDF text

| Method | Result |
|--------|--------|
| White shape in Word → PDF | Text often still extractable |
| White highlighter in Edge | Overlay only |
| White paint in design tool | Cosmetic cover |
| Change font color to white | Text still in content stream |

See [why blacking out a PDF is not safe](/blog/why-blacking-out-pdf-is-not-safe) — white overlays fail the same tests.
