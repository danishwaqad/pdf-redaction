## Quick answer

**iLovePDF** is excellent for merge, split, compress, and convert — not for professional **permanent redaction**. If you need to hide an SSN before email, the question is not brand familiarity but whether **Ctrl+F finds the string after you export**. Run that test on any tool's output. Permanent redaction is a **result**, not a marketing label.

## What iLovePDF is built for

iLovePDF's product surface area is general document utility: merge, split, compress, convert, sign, watermark. Those features assume you are **transforming** files, not **destroying specific text operators** under regulatory scrutiny.

Redaction relevance:

- **Merge / split** — unrelated to confidentiality
- **PDF to Word** — can **increase** leak risk if misused (editable text export)
- **Compress** — fine for non-sensitive files
- **Blackout-style markup** — may leave extractable text unless the product explicitly applies redaction semantics (verify with Ctrl+F)
