## Quick answer

**White out text in a PDF** is the same security job as blacking out: the sensitive string must be **removed from the file**, not covered with a white rectangle. A white shape in Word or Edge fails Ctrl+F the same way a black shape does. After [RedactPDF](/tool) apply, search the output — zero hits means you are done. See [black out text the right way](/blog/how-to-black-out-text-in-pdf) for the parallel workflow.

## Why people search "white out" instead of "black out"

Bank statements, medical forms, and visa packets often use white paper. Applicants want redacted regions to **blend in** rather than show harsh black bars. That is an aesthetic preference — not a different security model. Attackers do not care whether the overlay was white or black; they care whether the text layer still exists ([why blacking out fails](/blog/why-blacking-out-pdf-is-not-safe)).

A mortgage broker described the usual mistake: white rectangles over account numbers in a statement PDF exported from Word. The underwriter's system still indexed the full numbers. The fix was permanent redaction, not a different fill color.

## Wrong ways to white out PDF text

- White shape in Word or Google Docs → PDF export
- White highlighter in Edge or mobile markup
- Font color changed to white (text remains in content stream)
- Phone photo editor "eraser" on a screenshot

All fail the search-and-paste tests.

## What to white out on financial PDFs

Account numbers (often every page), full card numbers, unrelated transactions, internal reference codes. Usually **keep** name and salary deposits unless embassy guidance says otherwise. SSN handling: [redact SSN from PDF](/blog/how-to-redact-ssn-from-pdf).
