## Quick answer

A **scanned PDF** is often a picture — no selectable text, no PII auto-detect, no search until you **run OCR**. In [RedactPDF](/tool): upload → **Run OCR** on image pages → mark with detect + search + boxes → apply → verify. OCR does not replace permanent redaction; it unlocks marking. Full workflow: [redact PDF online free](/blog/how-to-redact-pdf-online-free).

## Scanned vs digital PDFs

Born-digital PDFs usually have text you can highlight. Fax exports, copier scans, and phone photos saved as PDF often do not. If the editor shows an OCR prompt or you cannot select words, treat the page as image-first.

**Hybrid PDFs** (Canva exports, some bank downloads) mix text and image layers — mark per page; use secure page mode on sensitive hybrid sheets when the whole background is sensitive.

## Step 1 — Run OCR

1. Open [RedactPDF](/tool) and upload the scan
2. On pages without text, tap **Run OCR**
3. Wait for recognition (engine loads from CDN — internet required)
4. Confirm search and highlights work when marking

Quality depends on resolution, skew, and handwriting. Garbled OCR needs **larger manual boxes** over whole lines. Handwriting recognition is limited — box the region visually.

## Step 2 — Mark sensitive content

After OCR:

- **PII auto-detect** for emails, phones, cards, SSN patterns — [auto redact guide](/blog/auto-redact-pdf-pii-detection)
- **Search** for names and case IDs OCR recognized
- **Manual boxes** on signatures, stamps, faces, barcodes, OCR misses

Zoom low-confidence regions. Headers repeat on every page — do not stop at page one.

## Step 3 — Apply and verify

Apply as with digital PDFs. On the **downloaded** file:

- Search known strings
- Try select under redacted areas
- Paste to Notepad

Failure means re-mark and re-apply. Why overlays fail: [why blacking out is not safe](/blog/why-blacking-out-pdf-is-not-safe).

## Common scan redaction mistakes

Black box in a photo editor without touching PDF structure. OCR skipped because "it looks like text." Assuming one page's OCR covers the whole file. Sharing before verification.
