## Quick answer

To **redact bank statement PDF** files for visa, mortgage, or landlord review: **permanently remove** account numbers, routing codes, and full card numbers — not white boxes in a phone app. Usually **keep** your name and salary deposits unless embassy guidance says otherwise. [RedactPDF](/tool) → mark → apply → **Ctrl+F** the account number on the download. White fill preference: [white out text guide](/blog/how-to-white-out-text-in-pdf).

> **Disclaimer:** Embassy and lender rules vary. Confirm what must stay visible before redacting. This is not immigration or financial advice.

## What applicants get wrong

A visa applicant white-boxed account numbers in Word and exported to PDF. The consulate's system still indexed full numbers from the text layer. Permanent redaction — not decoration — is what reviewers expect when they say "redact."

Wrong methods: phone photo stickers, Edge markup, Canva covers, cropped screenshots (often rejected for formatting). Right method: [remove text permanently](/blog/how-to-remove-text-from-pdf-permanently).

## What to redact (typical)

**Usually redact:** full account numbers (sometimes keep last four if required), routing/sort codes, full 16-digit card numbers, unrelated transactions (gambling, transfers not tied to the application), balances on accounts you are not proving.

**Usually keep:** account holder name and address (identity match), salary deposits and required proof-of-funds lines.

Rules differ by country — check current embassy or lender checklists.

## Step-by-step

1. Download the **official PDF** from your bank portal (not a screenshot)
2. Open [RedactPDF](/tool)
3. **Search** for your account number — mark all matches (headers repeat every page)
4. **PII detect** for cards and phones — [auto redact PII](/blog/auto-redact-pdf-pii-detection)
5. Box unrelated transaction lines manually
6. **Apply** permanent redaction
7. Verify: search account number, routing code, and a removed transaction string

Scanned statements need OCR first — [scanned PDF guide](/blog/how-to-redact-scanned-pdf-ocr).
