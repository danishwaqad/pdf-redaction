## Quick answer

To **black out text in a PDF** safely, you must **delete** the underlying text — not draw a black rectangle in Word, Canva, or Edge. After export, run **Ctrl+F** on a string you removed. If search still finds it, you have decoration, not redaction. Use [RedactPDF](/tool), apply permanent redaction, then verify. Read [why blacking out fails](/blog/why-blacking-out-pdf-is-not-safe) for the full picture.

## What people mean by "black out"

Colloquially, blacking out means "I should not see this anymore." Engineers hear "remove text operators from the content stream or rasterize the page." That gap causes breaches: a paralegal blacked out settlement numbers in a generic editor; opposing counsel searched the PDF and quoted the "hidden" figure in a hearing.

Wrong approaches:

- Black shape in Word → Save as PDF (text often extractable)
- Edge or Preview markup (overlays — see [Windows](/blog/redact-pdf-windows) and [Mac](/blog/redact-pdf-mac) guides)
- Screenshot with a bar pasted on top (original layer may survive)

Right approach: purpose-built redaction → apply → verify on the **downloaded** file.

## Step-by-step: black out text permanently

### 1. Open a redactor, not a layout editor

[RedactPDF](/tool) is built for permanent removal in the browser. Main walkthrough: [redact PDF online free](/blog/how-to-redact-pdf-online-free).

### 2. Scan the whole document

Headers and footers repeat case numbers and SSNs on every page. Tables hide account fragments in single cells. Annotations export as text. Mark each region — do not assume page one is enough.

### 3. Mark with boxes, search, and PII detect

Draw tight boxes on lines and paragraphs. **Search** for repeated names or matter IDs and use mark all matches. Run **PII detect** for emails, phones, cards, and SSN patterns ([auto redact guide](/blog/auto-redact-pdf-pii-detection)). Names still need manual search.

### 4. Apply permanent redaction

The output should show black or blank gaps where content was **removed**, not opaque stickers on live text.

### 5. Verify before email or filing

Search for known strings. Try to select under black areas. Paste the full document into Notepad. Any failure means do not send. Details: [remove text permanently](/blog/how-to-remove-text-from-pdf-permanently).

## Scanned pages

Image-only PDFs need **OCR** before search works — [scanned PDF guide](/blog/how-to-redact-scanned-pdf-ocr). Black boxes on bitmaps without OCR cleanup leave invisible text layers in many scans.

## Black out vs white out

For security, color does not matter — only removal does. Applicants sometimes prefer white fill on bank statements for aesthetics — see [white out text guide](/blog/how-to-white-out-text-in-pdf).
