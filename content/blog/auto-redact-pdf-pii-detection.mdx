## Quick answer

**Auto redact PDF** does not mean "click once and walk away." It means the tool **finds** emails, phones, SSN patterns, and cards — you **review every mark**, add what patterns miss, **apply** permanent redaction, then **verify** the download. [RedactPDF](/tool) PII panel + search handles the first pass; your eyes handle names, headers, and context. Hub: [redact PDF online free](/blog/how-to-redact-pdf-online-free).

## Why teams want auto redaction

A paralegal spent a Friday boxing emails in a 200-page FOIA production by hand. Monday, compliance found phones in footers the boxes missed. Auto-detect would have flagged most patterns in minutes — but only if someone still reviewed page by page before apply.

Automation saves time on **repetitive strings**. It does not replace judgment on **what** must go or **verification** after export.

## What RedactPDF auto-detects

The **PII panel** (desktop sidebar or mobile tabs) scans for:

- **Email** addresses
- **Phone** numbers (US and common international formats)
- **Credit card** 16-digit groups
- **Dates** in common layouts
- **SSN** patterns (###-##-####)

Each hit becomes a redaction mark you can accept, resize, or delete. Pair with **text search** for matter-specific IDs, docket numbers, and client names — regex will not catch "Acme Corp" unless you search it.

## The safe auto-redact workflow

1. Upload to [RedactPDF](/tool)
2. Run **PII detect** — scan marks on thumbnails
3. Run **search** for names, case numbers, custom strings
4. Manual pass: headers, footers, tables, signatures, images
5. **Apply** permanent redaction
6. **Verify**: Ctrl+F, select under boxes, paste to Notepad

Skip step 4 or 6 and you inherit liability. Details: [remove text permanently](/blog/how-to-remove-text-from-pdf-permanently).
